Selected for the IASc-INSA-NASI Summer Research Fellowship 2025, jointly awarded by the Indian Academy of Sciences, the Indian National Science Academy, and the National Academy of Sciences India.

Through the fellowship I interned at the Neurobehavioural laboratory at INMAS, DRDO Delhi, under Dr. Kailash Manda, studying the structural and neurobehavioural consequences of traumatic brain injury in rodent models using neurobehavioural paradigms alongside histopathological techniques.
