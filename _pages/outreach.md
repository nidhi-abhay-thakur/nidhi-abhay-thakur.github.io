## The NeuroReach Initiative

**Founder and Lead** · 2026 — Present

An independent outreach initiative to make brain science and brain health more accessible beyond the lab. Ongoing work includes collaborations with dementia care centres, educational media, and hospital outreach — through awareness campaigns and canvas painting donations to wards.

## Brain Matters — NISER's Neuroscience Club

**President** · May 2025 — Present

I lead the planning and execution of journal clubs, alumni sessions, live demonstrations, and talks by professors from India and abroad, building an active neuroscience community at NISER.
