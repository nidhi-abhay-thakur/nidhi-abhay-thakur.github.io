**Epilepsy Neurobiology Lab, AIIMS Delhi** · with Dr. Sarat P. Chandra and Dr. Jyotirmoy Banerjee · Summer 2026

Investigating how the ratio of estrogen to progesterone influences excitability in temporal lobe epilepsy — a question with direct bearing on catamenial epilepsy, where seizure frequency tracks the menstrual cycle.

The project combined work on resected human brain tissue with rodent model development, spanning several techniques:

- Processing of resected human brain tissue
- Patch clamp electrophysiology
- Epilepsy model development in rats
- ELISA for hormone quantification
- Video-EEG (VEEG) monitoring
- Histopathological assessment of samples
