**Synaptic Plasticity and Memory Lab, National University of Singapore** · with Dr. Sreedharan · Winter 2025

Recorded and analysed field excitatory post-synaptic potentials (fEPSPs) in rodent hippocampal slices to characterise plasticity impairments in models of Alzheimer's disease.

Long-term potentiation in the hippocampus is among the earliest processes to fail in Alzheimer's models, often before overt cell loss. Measuring fEPSPs in acute slices gives a direct readout of that synaptic failure and a way to test where along the pathway it begins.
