**Institute of Nuclear Medicine and Allied Sciences (INMAS), DRDO, Delhi** · with Dr. Kailash Manda · Summer 2025

Carried out through the [IASc-INSA-NASI Summer Research Fellowship 2025](https://www.iascs.ac.in/), a national fellowship jointly awarded by the three Indian science academies.

I investigated the structural and neurobehavioural consequences of traumatic brain injury in rodent models, pairing a battery of neurobehavioural paradigms with histopathological techniques so that behavioural deficits could be related back to tissue-level damage.
