**BITS Pilani, Goa Campus** · with Dr. Arnab Banerjee · Summer 2024

Studied the effect of the polyamine putrescine on the GnRH splice variant-1 (GnRH-V1) transcript and on the RNA-binding protein NOVA 1 in the hypothalamic GT1-7 cell line.

Putrescine treatment increased GnRH-V1 transcript expression relative to control without altering NOVA 1 expression — suggesting the splicing shift is not mediated by a change in NOVA 1 abundance. Because GnRH sits at the top of the reproductive endocrine axis and polyamine levels shift with age, the result has potential bearing on reproductive health, ageing, and neurodegeneration.

Conducted under the BITS Goa Summer Research Program 2024, one of 26 candidates selected nationwide. This work was later presented as a poster at the National Conference for Undergraduate Research in BioSciences (NCURB) at IISER Tirupati in December 2024.
