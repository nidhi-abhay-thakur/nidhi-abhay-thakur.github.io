I am a fourth-year Integrated MSc. student in Life Sciences at the National Institute of Science Education and Research (NISER), Bhubaneswar, supported by a DST-INSPIRE Fellowship.

My work sits at the intersection of synaptic plasticity, epilepsy, and brain injury. I have recorded field excitatory post-synaptic potentials in rodent hippocampal slices to probe plasticity impairments in Alzheimer’s disease models at the National University of Singapore; studied the structural and neurobehavioural consequences of traumatic brain injury at DRDO-INMAS, Delhi, as an IASc-INSA-NASI Summer Research Fellow; and investigated how the estrogen/progesterone ratio shapes temporal lobe epilepsy using resected human brain tissue and patch clamp at AIIMS Delhi. Earlier, at BITS Pilani Goa, I studied polyamine regulation of GnRH alternative splicing in the hypothalamic GT1-7 cell line.

Alongside research, I am President of Brain Matters, NISER’s neuroscience club, and founder of The NeuroReach Initiative, an outreach effort making brain science and brain health more accessible through dementia care centres, educational media, and hospital awareness campaigns.
